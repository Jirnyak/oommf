OOMMF

Object Oriented MicroMagnetic Framework

Open Source
Micromagnetics

Quantum Precision

OOMMF provides a powerful framework for simulating micromagnetic behavior. Developed primarily in the ITL/NIST, it enables researchers to compute complex magnetic states with high computational accuracy.

Extensible Architecture

Built with modularity in mind, OOMMF supports custom extensions and problem definitions. Its object-oriented nature makes it the go-to standard in the micromagnetics research community.
